Breakfast ناشتہ

The Lahori-morning table: puri puffed to order, paratha ghee-crisped on the tawa, paya and nihari slept overnight and woken at dawn. Salata, dahi and fresh naan ride along with everything.

02

Karahi کڑاہی

A karahi is a wok, not a curry — built to order on live fire and beaten through with tomato, ginger and whatever the school demands. Shinwari keeps it to salt and green chilli; the Lahori school lets the masala speak; the white school trades chilli-red for cream. Every karahi is finished with julienned ginger.
