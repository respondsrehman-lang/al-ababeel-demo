Our Story

A New Flame, Fed by Old Fires

Al Ababeel opened its doors in August 2026 carrying recipes older than most restaurants in Dubai — the charcoal craft of Kandahar, the pastry hands of Kabul, and the pulao patience that only a family kitchen can teach. We cook the way a mother does: slowly, generously, and without shortcuts.

Everything begins with the fire. Lamb and chicken meet real charcoal, never gas. Spices are pounded fresh each morning. The pulao steams under domed lids until every grain stands on its own — then it's showered with sweet carrots and raisins and set down in front of you like a gift.

This is Afghan hospitality — mehmani: the table never runs empty, the naan keeps coming, and you are somebody's guest here, not a table number.
